CROSSOVER BOXING

Introduction:

Crossover Boxing is a professional boxing bout that is intertwined with people who aren't necessarily known for boxing first, so people primarily known for things like music, acting, or just being influencers through YouTube or TikTok are the types of people we will find being in these fights. This idea was initially brought to light by Joe Weller on YouTube with his YouTube video "BOXING MATCH: Joe Weller Vs Malfoy" which led to a boxing match between Joe Weller and KSI on the 3rd of February 2018 which led to more fights making it more mainstream and popular bringing more eyes to the sport of boxing.
