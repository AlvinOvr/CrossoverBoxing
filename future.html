The Possible Future Of The Sport

As we've seen from the web pages before this crossover boxing has been around for a short period of time compared to mainstream boxing and within this time period it has become a staple in the world of combat sports, with the crossover boxing scene bringing in a new generation of fans. And with the scene being as volatile as it is there are limitless possibilities for the future of the scene as well as many potential risks that could bring about its downfall.

Looking below this passage on the future of the sport we can see some of the events that have already happened with some major ones being the three fights against boxing legends, two being against floyd mayweather and one being against Mike tyson. This shows there is no limit to what can happen in the scene that could bring the eyes of people to view it.

In the current state of crossover boxing everything greatly depends on very specific figures which is detrimental to its growth because if the scene strictly depends on these figures (KSI, Jake Paul, etc) when they inevitably retire the scene retires with them. Instead to ensure the longevity of the scene a couple of things need to be done one of them being cultivating new stars, the current big names in the scene are people who were stars before boxing through their youtube careers, and the already established stars never stop producing content even when they are training in fight camp, keeping their audience engaged even if the content output is less than if they were out of camp.

The scene could incorporate more fighters than majority influencers such as mma fighters potentially because if the fighter pool is limited to influencers eventually there will not be any more fighters to fight because everyone has fought everyone. It's also advised that they integrate with mainstream sport which promotion companies like MVP and Misfits Boxing do where they are seen working hand in hand with DZAN and other sports streaming services like the ufc to bring more eyes to the sport.

To conclude crossover boxing isn't just a joke to be dismissed and instead should be looked at as a gateway to gain new fans of the sport of boxing as a whole. As it grows it will continue to create its own legacy, connecting with a younger audience and with the potential to rival traditional boxing in terms of influence if it is built on the right foundation and takes the right steps to ensure its growth.
