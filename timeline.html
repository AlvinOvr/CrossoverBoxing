Joe Weller vs Theo Baker(2017)

On August 2,2017 a youtube video titled "Joe Weller vs Malfoy" was released by youtuber Joe Weller where he sparred fellow youtuber Theo Baker and after he won in that video he called out KSI which started the big boom of crossover boxing

KSI vs Joe Weller (February 3rd,2018)

This was the first major crossover boxing event, hosted in the copper box arena london, where KSI a major influencer in the UK won by TKO which established him as the face of crossover boxing.

KSI vs Logan Paul 1 (August 25th,2018)

After defeating Joe Weller, KSI called out Logan or Jake Paul, and Logan accepted. The fight took place on August 25, 2018, at Manchester Arena. At the time, Logan was facing backlash for his controversial Aokigahara Forest video, making him the villain in the eyes of many. Fans largely supported KSI, hoping he would win, but after six rounds, the fight ended in a majority draw. It is estimated that this fight gained about $8 million

Deji vs Jake paul(August 25th,2018)

Deji the younger brother of KSI and Jake Paul the younger brother of KSI had their own boxing mathch as the co-main event of KSI vs Logan Paul where Jake Paul won by TKO in the 5th round despite everyone believing that Deji would win the fight.

KSI vs Logan Paul 2 (November 29th 2019)

KSI and Logan Paul faced off in a highly anticipated rematch on November 9, 2019, at the Staples Center in Los Angeles. Unlike their first fight, this bout was a professional match, meaning no headgear and standard pro boxing rules. KSI started strong, while Logan had a key moment in Round 4, knocking KSI down but receiving a controversial two-point deduction for an illegal punch. This proved costly, as it resulted in a KSI victory in their rivalry. This fight earned an estimate of $14 million

Gib vs Jake Paul (January 2020)

AnEsonGib secured a bout against Jake Paul by aggressively calling him out after defeating Jay Swingler in the inaugural YouTube Boxing event (KSI vs. Logan Paul 1 undercard, 2018). With KSI defeating Logan Paul in their rematch, Gib viewed himself as the next to face Jake, and their battle was formally planned. The fight took place on January 30, 2020, in Miami, Florida, on the undercard of Demetrius Andrade vs. Luke Keeler. Gib entered with an unconventional approach but failed defensively due to the unusual footwork. Jake Paul dominated from the outset, landing three knockdowns in Round 1 until the referee called the bout off, giving Jake a first-round TKO victory. This victory aided Jake Paul's move into professional boxing.

Jake Paul vs Nate Robbinson (November 2020)

Jake Paul fought former NBA player Nate Robinson on November 28, 2020, on the undercard of Mike Tyson vs. Roy Jones Jr. The fight took place at the Staples Center in Los Angeles. Jake dominated from the start, knocking Robinson down multiple times before landing a brutal second-round KO, leaving Nate unconscious on the canvas. The knockout went viral, further boosting Jake Paul’s boxing career.

Jake Paul vs Ben Askren (April 2021)

Jake Paul faced veteran UFC fighter Ben Askren on April 17, 2021, in Atlanta, Georgia. Many people expected this to be Jake's most difficult battle because Askren was an experienced combat sports veteran and former welterweight champion in an MMA company. Despite his reputation for wrestling more than striking, many expected his expertise to give him an advantage over Jake, who was still regarded as an unknown boxer. Jake silenced the critics with a dominant performance, knocking out Askren in the first round with a clean right hand. The swift finish sparked debate, with some questioning Askren's conditioning and effort to the fight, but it solidified Jake Paul's image as a rising boxing star.
