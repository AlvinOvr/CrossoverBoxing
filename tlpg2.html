Social Gloves: YouTube vs TikTok (June 12, 2021)

On June 12, 2021, a boxing event was hosted by a newly established promotion company called Social Gloves, owned by YouTuber Austin McBroom. The goal was to bring in different audiences by getting creators from TikTok and YouTube to fight each other. This event featured major names such as Deji, Austin McBroom, AnEsonGib, Faze Jarvis (now Jarvis), Bryce Hall, and Tayler Holder. The event was widely discussed due to shocking results, including Faze Jarvis's dominant performance in his first-ever fight, Deji losing his fight in overwhelming fashion, and AnEsonGib losing in a controversial decision (later overturned). Financial controversies also followed the event.

Logan Paul vs Floyd Mayweather (June 6, 2021)

Logan Paul, a YouTuber, boxed legendary boxer Floyd Mayweather in an exhibition fight with no conclusive winner. This event further elevated influencers to a bigger platform, earning an estimated $100 million from sponsors, ticket sales, and PPV sales.

Jake Paul vs Tyron Woodley 1 & 2 (August 29 and December 18, 2021)

In August and December 2021, Jake Paul fought retired MMA fighter and former UFC welterweight champion Tyron Woodley. Jake won the first fight by split decision and the rematch by KO. Despite the wins, some fans believed both fights were rigged.

Showstar: UK vs USA (March 5, 2022)

This one-time event was hosted by Showstar Boxing, featuring UK influencers versus USA influencers. The main event was Deji vs Alex Wassabi, continuing the storyline of "Will Deji finally win a fight?" Deji lost again, while King Kenny lost to Faze Temperrr, and Salt Papi had a dominant win against Hallal Ham, establishing himself as a promising prospect in the scene.

Misfits Boxing 001: KSI vs Swarmz & Luis Pineda (August 27, 2022)

This event marked the launch of Misfits Boxing, a promotion company owned by KSI. KSI headlined the event, fighting both rapper Swarmz and professional boxer Luis Pineda in one night after his initial opponent, Alex Wassabi, pulled out due to injury. Other notable fights included Deji finally winning a fight against Fouseytube, Salt Papi securing another victory, King Kenny defeating Faze Sensei, Deen the Great winning his first Misfits fight, and Slim achieving a surprising victory against Faze Temperrr. After this Misfits have had multiple other events.

Jake Paul vs Tommy Fury (February 26th 2023)

This was a highly anticipated fight between Jake Paul and Tommy Fury, a professional boxer and brother of heavyweight champion Tyson Fury. The fight ended in a controversial split decision win for Tommy Fury, with many fans and analysts questioning the judges' scoring. This fight further solidified Jake Paul's position in the boxing world.

Kingpin Highstakes tournament (April 22 2023)

This was a highstakes tournament where we knew all the participants and the people they fought were randomly selected through a raffle on livestream. The first round of the tournament ended with Jarvis winning his fight against Tom Zanetti, AnEsonGib winning against Austin McBroom (owner of Social Gloves), King Kenny won against MyMateNate, and Winderson Nunes, a major Brazilian influencer, winning against Filip Marcinek. From the women, Elle Brooke won against Aleksandra Daniel, Jully Olivera known as Jully Poka won against Daniella Hemsley, Avery Pongracz known as 6AR6IE6 (Barbie) winning against Whitney Johns, and Emily Brooke winning against Amber O'Donnell.

Misfits 007: KSI vs Joe Fournier (May 13th 2023)

This was a controversial main event due to the age of KSI's opponent Joe Fournier. Besides this, KSI seemingly won by knockout, which was later changed due to KSI knocking Joe Fournier out with his elbow on accident. Besides that, we once again had Deji fighting and winning against Swarmz, along with Salt Papi losing against professional MMA fighter Anthony Taylor.

Kingpin Highstakes tournament 2 (June 15th 2023)

After the success of the first tournament, Kingpin decided to host a continuation of the tournament with the same rules. This round of the tournament ended up being the final round of the tournament due to controversies that came in after this round when it was leaked that most of the fighters hadn't gotten any payment from the previous tournament, along with many of the fighters choosing not to return. However, Gib came back to fight, winning in dominant fashion against Jarvis, King Kenny winning in dominant fashion against Winderson Nunes, and Jully Poka beating Elle Brooke in dominant fashion, bringing her to the spotlight as a big female fighter.

Misfits: THE PRIME CARD (October 14th 2023)

This card consisted of multiple fights with the main and co-main events being KSI vs Tommy Fury and Logan Paul vs Dillon Dannis. This ended with Logan Paul winning against Dillon Dannis due to Dillon getting disqualified for trying to grapple Logan, and KSI losing to Tommy Fury by unanimous decision with the audience being very dissatisfied because, from what the audience saw, Tommy didn't perform well enough to win, especially after the referee deducted a point from him. Besides the main two fights, we had Deen The Great vs Walid Sharks 2, a highly anticipated fight with Deen coming out victorious, and King Kenny vs Anthony Taylor resulting in King Kenny's loss.

Jake Paul vs Mike Tyson (November 15th 2024)

This fight was an extremely controversial fight because of the age gap between Jake Paul and Mike Tyson, being 31 years. The fight had a lot of eyes simply because of who Mike Tyson is, and people were expecting and hoping that he would win against Jake Paul. But in the end, Jake won by unanimous decision, causing Jake to receive a lot of backlash for fighting someone that old. However, it brought a lot of attention to Jake and gave him a bigger following.
